# Asset notes

The 48 bird images are individual AI-generated natural-history illustrations with transparent backgrounds. The maple, mahogany, and cherry textures are AI-generated wood studies. They are bundled as compressed WebP files and load from this site without external services. They are visual design references, not authoritative taxonomic plates or guaranteed samples of particular timber.

The original twelve species and arrangement follow the accompanying Figma design. Twenty additional birds include the Mourning dove requested earlier. Kōkako represents the original New Zealand blue-bird choice.

Sixteen more birds were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on October 17, 2026, in the same natural-history illustration style. Their transparent outputs are compressed to 600 × 600 WebP assets. The full prompt template, individual subject prompts, asset paths, and species-name reference links are recorded in `assets/bird-generation-2026-10.json`. These illustrations were generated individually, not retrieved from a dataset or copied from the reference sites.

Region labels identify a primary browsing association, not a species' complete geographic range. Bird groups are informal browsing categories rather than a taxonomic classification. Both filters derive their available choices from the catalogue, and user uploads appear under Custom.

Local typography: Cormorant Garamond and Inter, downloaded from Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font License texts are included in `assets/fonts/OFL-Cormorant-Garamond.txt` and `assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt`. Keep those license files with redistributed font assets.

User-uploaded artwork remains in browser storage and exported JSON. Use images you have permission to share.

The download panel can export a bird in PNG, JPEG, WebP, or SVG at its stored resolution. SVG files embed a PNG copy of the raster artwork and do not contain traced vector paths. JPEG uses a white background; PNG, WebP, and SVG retain any existing transparency. WebP originals are preserved byte-for-byte. ZIP collections include species names, file dimensions, and the hours assigned in the current design. Uploaded artwork is exported from its saved copy, resized to at most 512 px when uploaded.
